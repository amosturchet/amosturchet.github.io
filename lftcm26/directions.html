Il Palazzone di Cortona

The conference will take place at the historical villa "Palazzone di Cortona" of the Scuola Normale Superiore

The Palazzone is not a hotel, but rather a property of the Scuola Normale Superiore. It is a historic building and, as such is subject to specific regulations. One of these, on Sunday, the day of arrival, provides a special welcome service for guests staying at the Palazzone, with the following hours: 4:00 PM to 6:45 PM.

It is important to know that access to the Palazzone, its streets, gardens, and avenues is not permitted before 4:00 PM. Luggage storage is not available.

On Friday, September 11th, the Palazzone will close immediately after lunch.

Reaching Cortona and the Palazzone

Il Palazzone di Cortona is conveniently accessible by both air and rail. The nearest international airports are Florence Airport (approximately 120 km away) and Rome Fiumicino Airport (approximately 230 km away), both offering regular train connections to Camucia–Cortona (3 km) or Terentola-Cortona (7 km) stations via Florence or Rome. Both stations offer bus and taxi connections to Cortona. By car, the closest A1 highway exit is Val di Chiana (20 km). After the exit one can follow the signs for "Il Palazzone".
